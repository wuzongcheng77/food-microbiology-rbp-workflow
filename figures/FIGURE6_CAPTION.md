# Figure 6

Robustness of the frozen G_pre1 composite across the **complete 827-candidate universe**, version v5.4.8z2. (A) Q9T0Z9 rank after leaving out each scoring component and renormalizing the remaining weights. (B) Q9T0Z9 rank using each component alone; these are scoring-source baselines, not classifier performance or prediction accuracy. (C) Top5 inclusion frequency under 10,000 independent weight perturbations: each baseline weight was multiplied by an independent Uniform(0.8,1.2) factor, followed by renormalization (seed 20260906). All nine candidates with nonzero frequency are shown; the other 818 have zero frequency in the included source CSV. (D) Q9T0Z9 rank in the full, identifier-masked and strict-context-blind analyses.

Identifier masking removes identifiers and descriptive text from scoring inputs, but does not remove literature or target information already encoded in frozen numeric components. Strict context-blind additionally removes the target-context component and renormalizes the other three weights; literature-derived solubility evidence remains. Masking uses the frozen six-decimal components; baseline/ablation/perturbation preserve the original pre-rounding solubility calculation. Exact ties in sensitivity and masking analyses use seeded anonymous ordinals without secondary evidence terms. Full frozen reproduction retains the archived tie rule. All rank-based metrics and Top5/10 overlaps are in Supplementary Table S4; full rankings and all trial weights/margins are supplied.
